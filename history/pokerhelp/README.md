# PokerHelp

A beginner-friendly guide to the rules of poker.

## What is poker?

Poker is a card game where players try to make the best possible five-card hand, or to bluff opponents into thinking they have a stronger hand than they do. The game can be played casually at home or competitively in tournaments, but the core rules stay the same.

## Basic goal

Each player is dealt cards and then bets based on how strong their hand is. The best hand at showdown wins the pot, or a player can win by forcing all others to fold.

## Standard deck and hand ranking

Poker is usually played with a standard 52-card deck.

The card ranks, from highest to lowest, are:

- A, K, Q, J, 10, 9, 8, 7, 6, 5, 4, 3, 2

The suits are:

- Hearts
- Diamonds
- Clubs
- Spades

The suits are generally equal in value; only the rank and hand combination matter.

## Hand rankings

From strongest to weakest, the standard poker hands are:

1. Royal Flush
   - A, K, Q, J, 10 of the same suit
2. Straight Flush
   - Five consecutive cards of the same suit
3. Four of a Kind
   - Four cards of the same rank
4. Full House
   - Three of a kind plus a pair
5. Flush
   - Five cards of the same suit, not in sequence
6. Straight
   - Five consecutive cards of mixed suits
7. Three of a Kind
   - Three cards of the same rank
8. Two Pair
   - Two different pairs
9. One Pair
   - One pair of matching ranks
10. High Card
   - No matching combination; the highest card wins

### Notes on ties

- If two players have the same hand type, compare the highest matching cards first.
- If still tied, compare the next highest cards.
- In some poker variants, a straight ace-low (A-2-3-4-5) is allowed, but this is not always used in all games.

## How a hand is played

A typical poker hand has the following phases:

### 1. Deal

Players receive cards. In Texas Hold'em, each player gets two private cards. In other forms, players may be dealt a different number of cards.

### 2. Betting rounds

Players act in order, choosing from actions such as:

- Fold: give up the hand
- Check: pass the action without betting, if no one has bet yet
- Call: match the current bet
- Raise: increase the current bet
- All-in: bet all remaining chips

Betting continues until all active players have either matched the highest bet or folded.

### 3. Showdown

If more than one player remains after the final betting round, the players reveal their cards. The best hand wins the pot.

If a player bets and no one calls, they win the pot without showing their cards.

## Common poker variants

### Texas Hold'em

This is the most popular form of poker.

- Each player is dealt 2 private cards.
- Five community cards are revealed in stages: flop, turn, and river.
- Players use any combination of their two private cards and the five community cards to build the best five-card hand.

### Omaha

- Each player is dealt 4 private cards.
- Five community cards are used.
- Players must use exactly two of their hole cards and three community cards.

### Seven-Card Stud

- Each player is dealt 7 cards total.
- Some are face-up and some are face-down.
- Best five-card hand wins.

## Basic rules to remember

- Keep your cards hidden from others.
- Never reveal your hand before the showdown unless required by the rules.
- Understand the betting structure before starting a game.
- In most games, the dealer position rotates with each hand.
- Bluffing is legal, but you must follow table etiquette and house rules.

## Poker etiquette

- Be respectful to other players.
- Do not act out of turn.
- Do not touch your cards after betting in games where this is restricted.
- Keep the table friendly and fair.

## Quick summary

Poker is a game of chance, skill, and psychology. The goal is to build the best five-card hand or to convince others to fold. The better your understanding of hand rankings and betting flow, the stronger your decisions will be.
